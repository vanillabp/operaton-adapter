### A correlated message waits for its workflow for the message time-to-live, as on Camunda 8

Proposed by story 2060. Decided by the maintainer on 2026-10-30 and 2026-10-31.

The Camunda 7 adapter decides this in its own log, in the entry which story 2060 adds there.
Following decision 1 of this repository, that entry holds here unchanged, with Operaton as the
engine: `message-time-to-live` with the key, the meaning and the four levels of the Camunda 8
adapter; without it, or with zero, the refusal in phase one as in version 1; with it, phase one
checks only the model, phase two asks the outbox again through `PhaseTwoRetryLater` with the
deadline of the time-to-live, and a time-to-live which ran out ends with a
`PhaseTwoPermanentFailure` and a blocked entry. Nothing in Operaton needed a change of its own.
The code came over by hand, not through `bin/sync-from-camunda7.sh`, because the Camunda 7 change
was not merged yet. The next sync of that commit has to produce the same lines.

**Tests.** `OperatonMessageTimeToLiveIT` (Spring Boot), the time-to-live tests of
`OperatonWorkflowLifecycleTest` (Quarkus), `MessageTimeToLiveResolutionTest` and
`OperatonWaitForASubscriptionTest`.

**Where it is cited.** `README.md` (the list of keys) and the wiki pages `Configuration` and
`Transactional-behavior`, where they exist.
